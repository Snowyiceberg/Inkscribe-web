A Federal Heights Favorite for 17 Years

Tucked into the shopping center at 10250 Federal Blvd, Cleopatra Grill & Hummus has spent 17 years serving the food of Egypt and the Mediterranean — gyros, kabobs, shawarma, falafel, and hummus made from chickpeas, sesame sauce, lemon, garlic and olive oil.

Step inside and the theme surrounds you: pyramid paintings, a hand-painted mural of Cleopatra beneath a winged sun disc, and hieroglyphic borders framing the windows — the same artwork you’ll see on our storefront. Come hungry: plates arrive piled with golden rice, fresh salad, hummus and warm homemade pita.

17 Years in Business Means We’ve Done Something Right

Cleopatra Grill & Hummus is a family-owned restaurant that has been serving Federal Heights for 17 years. In a business where places come and go, 17 years of regulars, celebrations, and weeknight takeout orders means one thing: they’ve done something right.

Everything here is made fresh in-house, from scratch — house recipes, prepared in our own kitchen, nothing shipped in pre-made. That’s the Mediterranean and Middle Eastern cooking our guests come back for: grilled meats, homemade appetizers, and baklava in more varieties than you’d expect. The restaurant describes itself simply as “Denver’s best Middle Eastern food” — and after 17 years, plenty of Federal Heights agrees.

You’ll find us at 10250 Federal Blvd in the Federal Plaza Shopping Center, behind Chick-fil-A near 103rd and Federal. Come in for the food, stay for the artwork: pyramid paintings, Cleopatra herself beneath a winged sun disc, and hieroglyphic borders throughout the dining room.
