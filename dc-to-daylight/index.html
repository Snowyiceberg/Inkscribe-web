Our Story

DC to Daylight starts with a simple idea: a vintage computer isn't preserved until it's alive — powered up, booting its own software, with its manuals on the shelf beside it. A dead machine in a box is storage. A running machine with its documentation is history you can touch.

So each computer that comes into the project gets the full treatment: inspected, cleaned, repaired, and brought back to daylight — then catalogued here with its specifications, its manuals, its software library, and photographs of the actual machine, so the record survives even when the hardware is asleep.

The name is the job description. DC is where these machines live — direct current, power supplies, logic boards, the bench work. Daylight is where they're headed: switched on, screen lit, running again.
